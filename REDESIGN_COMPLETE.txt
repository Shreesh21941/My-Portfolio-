🎨 DESIGN CHANGES SUMMARY

✅ Color Scheme
   Before: Purple (#6366f1), Pink (#ec4899)
   After:  Deep Blue (#1e3a8a), Professional Blue (#3b82f6)
   Impact: More trustworthy, corporate appearance

✅ Navigation Bar
   Before: Frosted glass, rounded gradient logo
   After:  Clean white bar, solid logo, subtle border
   Impact: Minimal, professional navigation

✅ Typography
   Before: Large serif titles (Playfair, 3rem)
   After:  Modern sans-serif titles (Poppins, 2.5rem)
   Impact: More readable, contemporary feel

✅ Buttons & CTAs
   Before: Gradient buttons with large shadows
   After:  Solid blue buttons with subtle shadows
   Impact: Cleaner, more professional appearance

✅ Cards & Components
   Before: 15px radius, large shadows, colorful backgrounds
   After:  8px radius, subtle shadows, left border accents
   Impact: Minimalist, elegant, professional

✅ Section Backgrounds
   Before: Colorful gradients throughout
   After:  White/light gray professional palette
   Impact: Better visual hierarchy

✅ Shadows & Depth
   Before: Large prominent shadows (--shadow-lg, --xl)
   After:  Subtle minimal shadows (--shadow-sm, --md)
   Impact: Modern flat design with subtle depth

✅ Hover Effects
   Before: Large transforms (5-10px), dramatic changes
   After:  Subtle transforms (2-3px), refined feel
   Impact: Polished, professional interactions
